Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

This app is a personal dashboard run by Floo ("we", "us"). It shows the owner's own social media statistics on our website.

2. What data we access

With the account owner's permission, the app reads data from the owner's own TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube accounts through their official APIs: follower or subscriber counts, and view, like, comment, and share counts for recent videos. It does not access private messages, passwords, or data about other users.

3. What we store and show

The app saves only summary numbers (such as total followers and views) in a public file used to display the dashboard. Login tokens issued by the platforms are kept in private encrypted storage and are never published.

4. Website visitors

Visitors can view the dashboard without logging in. The dashboard does not ask visitors for personal information and does not set tracking cookies of its own. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may collect standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. The summary statistics are public by design, since they are displayed on our website.

6. Retention and deletion

Statistics history is kept for roughly six months. The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the settings of each platform (TikTok, Instagram, or Google), and we will delete stored tokens on request.

7. Changes

We may update this policy, and the date above will change when we do.
